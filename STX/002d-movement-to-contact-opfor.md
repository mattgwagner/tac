# OPFOR CARD — 002D ROLLING THUNDER

**For the OPFOR team only. Do not share with the squad being evaluated.**

**See also:** [Detailed OPORD](002d-movement-to-contact-detailed.html)

---

## Your job

**You are a training aid, not an adversary.** The squads being evaluated are learning to lead — their SLs are being graded on planning, movement techniques, actions on contact, and battle drill execution. Your job is to give them a scripted enemy to react to, not to outthink them or "win" the engagement. **Stick to the card.** No freelancing, no flanking, no clever tactics unless cadre injects one.

If the squad leader's plan is solid, it should work. If it's not, cadre will make that call — not you.

---

**You are:** WRAITH security elements screening assigned zones in AO STEELE. Three independent elements, one per zone. **Elements do NOT coordinate with each other.**

**Weapons (all elements):** M4 with blanks. Small arms only.

## Element WEST (OBJ IRON)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0600 1538 (Bradenton Ave intersection)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Tactic: Screen and delay.** Observe the friendly approach from concealment. When friendlies get within 50m, fire 1-2 short bursts to alert and delay, then bound back 30-40m. Re-engage with 1 more short burst, then break contact **north**. WRAITH screens — it does not hold ground.

## Element CENTRAL (OBJ STEEL)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0612 1546 (road junction near FOB)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Tactic: Hasty ambush then withdraw.** Occupy concealed positions along the approach. Initiate at 30m, fire for 10-15 seconds, then immediately break contact **north**. WRAITH does not linger — one engagement and gone.

## Element EAST (OBJ COPPER)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0613 1532 (Conex City structures)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Tactic: Observe and delay.** Use the structures for observation. When friendlies approach within 50m, fire 1 burst from the structure entrance, then withdraw **north through** the structure and break contact. Do NOT hold the structure.

## General rules

- **You are a training aid, not an adversary.** Do not try to "win."
- WRAITH fights briefly and withdraws — **no prolonged stands**.
- No flanking, bounding, or out-of-the-box tactics unless the card or cadre says so.
- Do not pursue if they break contact.
- If your element takes "casualties" (cadre call), go down and stay down.
- Each element operates independently — **do not reinforce each other**.
- Stay in character. Do not coach or critique.
- Police your brass before leaving your position.
- Be safe with blanks — 5m minimum engagement distance, muzzle awareness always.
