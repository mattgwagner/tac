# OPFOR CARD — 003D RAPID STRIKE

**For the OPFOR team only. Do not share with the squad being evaluated.**

**See also:** [Detailed OPORD](003d-raid-a-bunker-detailed.html)

---

## Your job

**You are a training aid, not an adversary.** The squads being evaluated are learning to plan and execute a squad raid. Your job is to give them a scripted enemy to react to — not to outthink them or "win." **Stick to the card.** No freelancing unless cadre says so.

If the squad leader's plan is solid, it should work. If it's not, cadre will make that call — not you.

---

**You are:** Three independent WRAITH intelligence collection nodes distributed across AO CEDAR. **Nodes do NOT coordinate with each other.**

**Weapons (all nodes):** M4 with blanks. Small arms only.

## Node 1 — OBJ NEXUS (1st SQD)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0600 1538 (Bradenton Ave / unnamed E-W road intersection)
- **Type:** WRAITH terrain analysis team
- **Size:** 2-4 personnel
- **Posture:** Working position at the junction using woodline for concealment. 1-2 analysts with maps and notebooks, 1-2 security oriented south. Look focused on work, not combat-postured.
- **Props:** Annotated printed maps, notebook with terrain sketches, binoculars.
- **When contact begins:**
  1. **First priority:** Analysts attempt to tear and destroy the annotated maps and notebook
  2. Security returns fire from their positions for 15 seconds
  3. Do NOT maneuver out — fight from where you are
  4. When the assault closes to 15m, take "casualties" on cadre call
  5. Leave partially destroyed maps and the binoculars behind for SSE

## Node 2 — OBJ VERTEX (2nd SQD)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0613 1532 (Conex City NE building)
- **Type:** WRAITH analytical cell
- **Size:** 2-4 personnel
- **Posture:** Inside the NE structure. Analysts at a table or desk with maps spread out, security at entrances.
- **Props:** Multiple annotated maps, a bound notebook, printed imagery, a clipboard with notes.
- **When contact begins:**
  1. Analysts immediately roll up and stuff the most sensitive maps and the notebook into a bag — they can take 10-15 seconds on this
  2. Security fires from the doorway for 15-20 seconds
  3. Do NOT exit to maneuver
  4. When the assault reaches the door, take "casualties" on cadre call
  5. Leave the clipboard and loose maps behind — the bound notebook is "mostly destroyed"

## Node 3 — OBJ APEX (3rd SQD)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0575 1524 (Arcadia St / Clearwater Ave junction)
- **Type:** WRAITH terrain survey team
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Posture:** Survey position near the junction. Binoculars, sketch pad, handheld radio. Analysts sketching terrain features, 1 security oriented south.
- **Props:** Binoculars, sketch pad with terrain drawings, handheld radio, a compass.
- **When contact begins:**
  1. Analyst grabs the sketch pad and attempts to fold/pocket it
  2. Return limited fire (3-5 rounds each) — WRAITH is not primarily fighters
  3. Break contact quickly — within 10 seconds of initiating fire. Withdraw east.
  4. Leave binoculars, compass, and handheld radio behind

## Do NOT (all nodes)

- Maneuver out of your position (except Node 3's quick break-contact)
- Pursue withdrawing friendlies
- Reinforce another node
- Successfully destroy ALL materials — the SSE requires some captured intelligence

## General rules

- **You are a training aid, not an adversary.** Do not try to "win."
- Stay in character. Do not coach or critique.
- Police your brass before leaving your position.
- Be safe with blanks — 5m minimum engagement distance, muzzle awareness always.
