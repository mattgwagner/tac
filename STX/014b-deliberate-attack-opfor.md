# OPFOR CARD — 014B IRON FIST

**For the OPFOR team only. Do not share with the squad being evaluated.**

**See also:** [Detailed OPORD](014b-deliberate-attack-detailed.html)

---

## Your job

**You are a training aid, not an adversary.** The squads being evaluated are learning to lead — each SL is being graded on planning, assault coordination, and conducting a hasty Sensitive Site Exploitation (SSE) of the coordination center. Your job is to give them a scripted enemy to react to, not to outthink them or "win" the engagement. **Stick to the card.** No freelancing, no flanking, no clever tactics unless cadre injects one.

Three squads will execute simultaneously, one per objective. Cadre assigns one OPFOR set per objective. If manning is limited, rotate one set between iterations.

---

**You are:** Three independent COBRA coordination centers distributed across AO STARKE. **Elements do NOT coordinate with each other.**

**Weapons (all elements):** M4s with blanks. Small arms only.

**Props (all elements):** Handheld radio with antenna extended, message log (printed pages), map with markings, clipboard with notes.

---

## OBJ STATION (1st Squad's Objective)

- **Location:** Vic MP 0600 1523 (Arcadia St / Bradenton Ave junction)
- **Size:** 3-4 personnel — 1-2 operators at a field desk or ground cloth, 2 security in fighting positions oriented south and southwest

### CP Operators (1-2 personnel)

- Seated with map, clipboard, and radio — act like you're managing communications and message traffic
- Orient toward the southern approach, NOT the western woodline
- **Weapons slung or set aside** — you are coordination staff, not fighters

**When contact begins:**
1. **First priority:** Destroy message logs and schedules — crumple, tear, or bag them to simulate burning
2. **Second priority:** Transmit one radio message: "STATION under attack, requesting support"
3. Defend yourselves only if directly threatened at close range
4. Take "casualties" on cadre call when the assault element arrives
5. **Leave some materials** — the SSE exercise requires captured documents

### Security Element (2 personnel)

- Occupy fighting positions south and southwest of the coordination center
- When contact begins: return fire from your positions, oriented south/southwest, for 30 seconds
- Hold until assault element closes to 15m, then take "casualties" on cadre call
- Do NOT maneuver out of your positions or reinforce the operators

## OBJ NEXUS (2nd Squad's Objective)

- **Location:** Vic MP 0602 1565 (Bradenton Ave / Jacksonville St junction)
- **Size:** 3-4 personnel — 1-2 operators at a portable table or ground cloth, 2 security oriented south along Bradenton Ave

### CP Operators

- Same working posture as OBJ STATION
- Dense woods to the north are your notional withdrawal route — acknowledge in character if pressed, but operators do not successfully escape

**When contact begins:** Same priority sequence — destroy documents → transmit warning → defend as last resort → take "casualties" on cadre call.

### Security Element

- Oriented south along Bradenton Ave
- Same fight-from-positions script — hold 30 seconds, take "casualties" at 15m

## OBJ HUB (3rd Squad's Objective)

- **Location:** Vic MP 0611 1530 (Conex City center)
- **Size:** 3-4 personnel — 1-2 operators inside a structure working from a table, 2 security at the entrances

### CP Operators

- Working inside the structure with props spread on the table
- Security at the entrances provides early warning — operators begin destroying materials at first sound of contact

**When contact begins:** Same priority sequence — destruction → transmission → defend as last resort → casualties on cadre call.

### Security Element

- Positioned at structure entrances
- Hold briefly (15-20 seconds) when contact begins, then take "casualties" on cadre call as the assault element reaches the entrance

## Do NOT (all elements)

- Maneuver out of prepared positions
- Pursue the assault element
- Successfully destroy ALL materials — leave something for SSE
- Counterattack from the flank (unless cadre activates MDCOA inject)

## OPTIONAL MDCOA inject (cadre activates only)

- 2-3 additional OPFOR at one objective positioned in the woodline off the flank
- On cadre signal, open fire from an unexpected direction against the assault element
- CP operators successfully transmit warning and destroy all documents before being overrun

## General rules

- **You are a training aid, not an adversary.** Do not try to "win."
- No flanking, bounding, or out-of-the-box tactics unless the card or cadre says so.
- Stay in character. Do not coach or critique.
- Police your brass before leaving your position.
- Be safe with blanks — 5m minimum engagement distance, muzzle awareness always.
