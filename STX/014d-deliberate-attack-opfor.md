# OPFOR CARD — 014D WRAITH HUNT

**For the OPFOR team only. Do not share with the squad being evaluated.**

**See also:** [Detailed OPORD](014d-deliberate-attack-detailed.html)

---

## Your job

**You are a training aid, not an adversary.** The squads being evaluated are learning to lead — each SL is being graded on planning, assault speed, and conducting an SSE of WRAITH planning materials. Your job is to give them a scripted enemy to react to, not to outthink them or "win." **Stick to the card.**

Three squads will execute simultaneously, one per objective. Cadre assigns one OPFOR set per objective. If manning is limited, rotate one set between iterations.

**The training tension is assault speed.** WRAITH planners methodically destroy the operational plans that are the mission's intelligence prize. A fast, well-coordinated assault catches the plans intact. A slow or disorganized assault arrives to find ashes. **Cadre controls how much destruction occurs before the assault arrives.**

---

**You are:** Three independent WRAITH planning cells distributed across AO CEDAR. **Elements do NOT coordinate with each other.**

**Weapons (all elements):** M4s with blanks. Small arms only.

**Props (all elements):** Annotated maps with overlays, bound notebooks labeled as WRAITH operational plans, a bag or similar item to simulate burning documents. The plans are the prize — handle them prominently so the squad understands what they're racing to capture.

## OBJ SIGMA (1st Squad's Objective)

- **Location:** Vic MP 0600 1523 (Arcadia St / Bradenton Ave junction)
- **Size:** 3-4 personnel — 1-2 planners at a ground cloth or field desk, 2 security in fighting positions oriented south and southwest

### Planners (1-2 personnel)

- Seated at ground cloth studying maps and writing in notebooks — you are doing serious operational planning work
- **Weapons slung or set aside** — you are intelligence professionals, not fighters
- **When contact begins:** Begin destroying documents immediately and methodically — operational plans first, map overlays second, notebooks last
- WRAITH planners do NOT attempt to escape — hold your position and protect your work to the end
- Take "casualties" on cadre call when the assault element reaches the planning site

### Security Element (2 personnel)

- Occupy fighting positions south and southwest — you are buying time for the planners
- **When contact begins:** Return deliberate, steady fire — controlled suppression, not a panicked spray
- Hold from your positions until the assault element closes to 15m, then take "casualties" on cadre call
- Do NOT maneuver forward or vacate your positions — hold and absorb the assault

## Cadre: Controlling Document Destruction

- **WALK iterations:** Planners are slow to start — most documents captured intact. Trains the squad on a full, successful SSE.
- **RUN iterations:** Planners begin destruction immediately; cadre calls "destroyed" on the most sensitive docs as time passes. Trains the squad on partial SSE and the cost of a delayed assault.
- Never destroy everything — leave enough for a meaningful SSE and intelligence report.

## Do NOT (all elements)

- Planners attempt to escape — they hold their position
- Security pursues the assault element
- Reinforce another element
- Counterattack from the flank (unless cadre activates MDCOA inject)

## OPTIONAL MDCOA inject (cadre activates only)

- 2-3 additional WRAITH positioned in the flank woodline, 50-75m away
- On cadre signal, open fire against the assault element's flank as they reach the objective
- Planners destroy all operational plans before the assault arrives

## General rules

- **You are a training aid, not an adversary.** Do not try to "win."
- No flanking, bounding, or out-of-the-box tactics unless the card or cadre says so.
- Stay in character. Do not coach or critique.
- Police your brass before leaving your position.
- Be safe with blanks — 5m minimum engagement distance, muzzle awareness always.
