**For the OPFOR team only. Do not share with the squad being evaluated.**

## Your job

**You are a training aid, not an adversary.** The squad being evaluated is learning to lead — their SL is being graded on planning, SBF coordination, and assault execution. Your job is to give them a scripted enemy to react to, not to outthink them or "win" the engagement. **Stick to the card.**

Three squads will execute simultaneously, one per objective. Cadre assigns one OPFOR set per objective. If manning is limited, rotate one set between iterations.

**WRAITH is patient, disciplined, and does not die on hills.** The training tension here is observation post detection and approach discipline. WRAITH's OP is further out than usual and goes silent before breaking contact — so the squad may not know it's been detected until the main body opens fire. If the squad is sloppy on approach, they walk into a prepared position at close range.

---

**You are:** Three independent WRAITH security cordon elements distributed across AO STEELE. **Elements do NOT coordinate with each other.**

**Weapons (all elements):** M4s with blanks. Small arms only. No support weapon — WRAITH fires briefly to disengage, not to win a firefight.

## OBJ CROSSBOW (1st Squad's Objective)

**Location:** Vic MP 0600 1538 (Bradenton Ave / unnamed E-W road junction)

**Main body (3-4 personnel)**
- Occupy fighting positions on the **north side of the junction**, oriented **south and east**
- Patient and still — WRAITH does not fidget or make noise
- Weapons at the ready, not slung

**Observation Post (1-2 personnel)**
- Position approximately **300m south** along Bradenton Ave (vic MP 0600 1508)
- **Go silent** when you detect the friendly squad — do NOT fire immediately
- Transmit one quiet radio message to the main body: "Movement south, grid ____"
- Wait for the squad to move past or within 50m, then break contact **north silently** — no firing unless directly engaged
- If directly engaged at close range: return 2-3 rounds maximum, then break contact north

**When the main fight starts**
1. Main body returns deliberate, controlled fire — not a heavy volume, but accurate and sustained for 20-30 seconds
2. Fire is the rearguard — WRAITH buys enough time to withdraw, not enough to "win"
3. Hold until assault element closes to 15m, then take "casualties" on cadre call
4. Withdraw **north into dense woods** immediately — WRAITH does not contest the objective once the assault arrives

## Detection guidance (all elements)

- The OP's job is to detect, not engage. **Going silent is the hardest thing to do — and the most important.**
- If the friendly squad is very cautious and quiet, the OP may genuinely not detect them. That is a training success — stay in character.
- Cadre will signal when the squad has been detected by the OP if it is unclear.

## Do NOT (all elements)

- The OP fires first at long range — WRAITH goes silent and withdraws
- Main body fires heavy volume — deliberate, controlled suppression only
- Pursue the assault element
- Counterattack from the flank (unless cadre activates MDCOA inject)

## OPTIONAL MDCOA inject (cadre activates only)

- WRAITH OP detects the squad early and passes a precise grid
- Main body repositions slightly before the assault to engage from an unexpected angle
- On cadre signal, WRAITH withdraws entirely before the assault arrives — objective is empty; squad must report and consolidate on an uncontested position

## General rules

- **You are a training aid, not an adversary.** Do not try to "win."
- No flanking, bounding, or out-of-the-box tactics unless the card or cadre says so.
- Stay in character. Do not coach or critique.
- Police your brass before leaving your position.
- Be safe with blanks — 5m minimum engagement distance, muzzle awareness always.
