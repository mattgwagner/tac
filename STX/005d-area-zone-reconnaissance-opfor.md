# OPFOR CARD — 005D DEEP RECON

**For the OPFOR team only. Do not share with the squad being evaluated.**

**See also:** [Detailed OPORD](005d-area-zone-reconnaissance-detailed.html)

---

## Your job

**You are a training aid, not an adversary.** The squads being evaluated are learning to plan and execute a squad area reconnaissance — moving covertly, establishing observation, and reporting without being detected. Your job is to be a realistic enemy presence, not to hunt the squad or "win." **Stick to the card.**

The squad's goal is to observe you without being seen. If they do it well, you may not know they were there. That's a successful training lane. **WRAITH is the hardest to detect and the quickest to disengage — make the observation challenge genuine.**

---

**You are:** Three independent WRAITH observation post elements distributed across AO ARCHER. **Elements do NOT coordinate with each other.**

**Weapons (all elements):** M4 with blanks. Small arms only.

**Props:** Each element has binoculars, stakes or string for OP marking, and a handheld radio. These represent OP construction materials.

**Posture:** Extremely still and patient. WRAITH is purpose-built for surveillance — they scan slowly, remain motionless for long periods, and use camouflage. This is the hardest element to observe without being detected.

## Element EAST (NAI SPECTER)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0613 1532 (Conex City NE building)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Activity:** 1 person with binoculars scanning to the south from a concealed position. 1 person constructing or improving the OP (staking position, adding camouflage). 1 security facing away from the building.
- **If observed without being detected:** Stay in character. Continue OP activity.
- **If squad is detected:** Return **no fire initially** — WRAITH transmits one radio message ("Contact, grid ____") then breaks contact silently **north** through the structure. If directly engaged, return limited fire (2-3 rounds) then break contact. Do NOT pursue.

## Detection guidance (all elements)

- WRAITH is extremely difficult to detect. Stay still, scan slowly, use cover properly.
- Normal security: scan your sector every 30 seconds with deliberate, slow movement.
- If you hear or see movement within 15m, transmit a report first, then react per above.
- Do NOT call out or challenge — WRAITH goes silent when it detects a threat.
- Cadre will signal when a squad has been detected if it's unclear.

## General rules

- **You are a training aid, not an adversary.** Do not try to "win."
- WRAITH disengages — it does not fight for the OP position.
- Do NOT pursue the reconnaissance squad under any circumstances.
- Do NOT reinforce another element.
- Stay in character. Do not coach or critique.
- Police your brass before leaving your position.
- Be safe with blanks — 5m minimum engagement distance, muzzle awareness always.
