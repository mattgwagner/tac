# OPFOR CARD — 005C SHADOW RECON

**For the OPFOR team only. Do not share with the squad being evaluated.**

**See also:** [Detailed OPORD](005c-area-zone-reconnaissance-detailed.html)

---

## Your job

**You are a training aid, not an adversary.** The squads being evaluated are learning to plan and execute a squad area reconnaissance — moving covertly, establishing observation, and reporting without being detected. Your job is to be a realistic enemy presence, not to hunt the squad or "win." **Stick to the card.**

The squad's goal is to observe you without being seen. If they do it well, you may not know they were there. That's a successful training lane.

---

**You are:** Three independent VIPER staging elements distributed across AO CLAY. **Elements do NOT coordinate with each other.**

**Weapons (all elements):** M4 with blanks. Small arms only.

**Posture:** Active, not combat-postured, but more alert than COBRA or WRAITH. VIPER personnel move around more, talk to each other, and are generally noisier — which actually makes them easier to detect from a distance but also means they may notice nearby movement more readily.

## Element EAST (NAI FOXHOUND)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0613 1532 (Conex City NE building)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Activity:** Moving around the structure, checking it out, possibly sitting nearby. Active and somewhat careless.
- **If observed without being detected:** Stay in character.
- **If squad is detected:** VIPER fires immediately and aggressively. Return fire toward the squad, advance 10-15m while firing, then break contact **north**. VIPER reacts — it doesn't just run. Do NOT pursue more than 15m.

## Element NORTH (NAI VIXEN)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0602 1565 (Bradenton Ave / Jacksonville St intersection)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Activity:** Resting or moving near the intersection woodline. Periodically moving to the road to look north and south.
- **If observed without being detected:** Stay in character.
- **If squad is detected:** Return aggressive fire and advance 10m toward the contact, then break contact **north**. Do NOT pursue further.

## Element SOUTHWEST (NAI DINGO)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0575 1524 (Arcadia St / Clearwater Ave intersection)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Activity:** Resting or moving near the junction. Less disciplined security than the COBRA or WRAITH elements.
- **If observed without being detected:** Stay in character.
- **If squad is detected:** Return fire aggressively and break contact **west** into the woodline after 10-15 seconds. Do NOT pursue.

## Detection guidance (all elements)

- VIPER is noisier and less disciplined — more likely to be detected at distance, but also reacts more aggressively when the squad is detected.
- Normal security: scan every 60-90 seconds. VIPER is confident the area is theirs.
- If you hear movement within 25m, fire immediately without challenging — VIPER is trigger-happy.
- Cadre will signal when a squad has been detected if it's unclear.

## General rules

- **You are a training aid, not an adversary.** Do not try to "win."
- Do NOT pursue the reconnaissance squad more than 15m.
- Do NOT reinforce another element.
- Stay in character. Do not coach or critique.
- Police your brass before leaving your position.
- Be safe with blanks — 5m minimum engagement distance, muzzle awareness always.
