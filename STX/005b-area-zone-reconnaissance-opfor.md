# OPFOR CARD — 005B SILENT STALK

**For the OPFOR team only. Do not share with the squad being evaluated.**

**See also:** [Detailed OPORD](005b-area-zone-reconnaissance-detailed.html)

---

## Your job

**You are a training aid, not an adversary.** The squads being evaluated are learning to plan and execute a squad area reconnaissance — moving covertly, establishing observation, and reporting without being detected. Your job is to be a realistic enemy presence, not to hunt the squad or "win." **Stick to the card.**

The squad's goal is to observe you without being seen. If they do it well, you may not know they were there. That's a successful training lane.

---

**You are:** Three independent COBRA logistics elements distributed across AO KINGSLEY. **Elements do NOT coordinate with each other.**

**Weapons (all elements):** M4 with blanks. Small arms only.

**Posture:** Working, not combat-postured. You are maintaining cache materials, monitoring movement, or waiting for couriers. You are not expecting contact.

## Element EAST (NAI CONDOR)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0613 1532 (Conex City NE building)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Activity:** One or two personnel near the structure entrance or inside. Periodically checking surroundings but not actively patrolling.
- **If observed without being detected:** Stay in character. Let the squad observe and withdraw.
- **If squad is detected:** Return limited fire, then immediately break contact **north through** Conex City and withdraw. Do NOT pursue.

## Element NORTH (NAI OSPREY)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0602 1565 (Bradenton Ave / Jacksonville St intersection)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Activity:** Positioned in woodline near the intersection. One person occasionally stepping to the intersection to look both ways, then returning to cover. Relaxed posture.
- **If observed without being detected:** Stay in character.
- **If squad is detected:** Return limited fire and break contact **north** along the woodline. Do NOT pursue.

## Element SOUTHWEST (NAI RAPTOR)

- **Location:** Vicinity MP 0575 1524 (Arcadia St / Clearwater Ave intersection)
- **Size:** 2-3 personnel
- **Activity:** Concealed in vegetation near the intersection. Static, observing approaches from the south.
- **If observed without being detected:** Stay in character.
- **If squad is detected:** Return limited fire and break contact **west** into the woodline. Do NOT pursue.

## Detection guidance (all elements)

- Make detection realistic but not automatic. Don't stare at woodlines looking for the squad.
- Normal security: scan your area every 30-60 seconds. Don't specifically hunt for the recon team.
- If you hear movement within 20m, challenge (one call) then react per above.
- Cadre will signal when a squad has been detected if it's unclear.

## General rules

- **You are a training aid, not an adversary.** Do not try to "win."
- Do NOT pursue the reconnaissance squad.
- Do NOT reinforce another element.
- Stay in character. Do not coach or critique.
- Police your brass before leaving your position.
- Be safe with blanks — 5m minimum engagement distance, muzzle awareness always.
