# OPFOR CARD — 001B DARBY'S RAIDERS

**For the OPFOR team only. Do not share with the squad being evaluated.**

**See also:** [Detailed OPORD](001b-ambush-detailed.html)

---

## Your job

**You are a training aid, not an adversary.** The squads being evaluated are learning to lead — their SLs are being graded on planning ambushes, site selection, initiation, and break-contact. Your job is to give them a scripted enemy to react to, not to outthink them or "win" the engagement. **Stick to the card.** No freelancing, no flanking, no clever tactics unless cadre injects one.

If the squad leader's plan is solid, it should work. If it's not, cadre will make that call — not you.

---

**You are:** Three independent COBRA courier teams transiting AO KINGSLEY. **Teams do NOT coordinate with each other.** One team per ambush site; if manning is limited, rotate a single OPFOR team across sites between iterations.

**Weapons (all teams):** M4 with blanks. Small arms only.

**Props (optional):** Each team may carry a rucksack or wrapped bundle to represent courier cargo/documents. Leave it behind on the objective — it's the SSE take.

## Team WEST (OBJ MERRILL)

- **Size:** 3-4 personnel
- **Starting location:** Vicinity MP 0598 1525 (south end of Bradenton Ave)
- **Movement:** North along Bradenton Ave toward MP 0600 1530 at a deliberate pace, 5-10m spacing, moving like couriers on a known-safe route — weapons slung or low-ready, not tactically alert
- **When ambushed:** Return 2-3 rounds each of limited, inaccurate fire, take cover for 5-10 seconds, then break contact **south** along Bradenton — do not fight through. Take "casualties" on cadre call. Leave the "cargo bundle" behind.

## Team NORTHWEST (OBJ TARAWA)

- **Size:** 3-4 personnel
- **Starting location:** Vicinity MP 0585 1565 (north of OBJ TARAWA on the unnamed N-S road in the western LTA)
- **Movement:** South along the unnamed road toward MP 0585 1559 at a deliberate pace, 5-10m spacing
- **When ambushed:** Return limited fire, take cover briefly, then break contact **north** back up the road. Take "casualties" on cadre call. Leave the "cargo bundle" behind.

## Team SOUTHWEST (OBJ RODGERS)

- **Size:** 3-4 personnel
- **Starting location:** Vicinity MP 0582 1538 (east of OBJ RODGERS along the unnamed E-W road)
- **Movement:** West along the road toward the Clearwater Ave intersection at MP 0575 1538 at a deliberate pace
- **When ambushed:** Return limited fire, take cover briefly, then break contact **east** back along the road. Take "casualties" on cadre call. Leave the "cargo bundle" behind.

## Do NOT (all teams)

- Flank the ambush element
- Fight effectively — you are supposed to get ambushed
- Call for reinforcement
- Reinforce another site

## General rules (all OPFOR)

- **You are a training aid, not an adversary.** Do not try to "win."
- No flanking, bounding, or out-of-the-box tactics unless the card or cadre says so.
- Each team operates independently — **do not coordinate with the other teams**.
- Stay in character. Do not coach or critique the squad during the lane.
- Police your brass before leaving your position.
- Be safe with blanks — 5m minimum engagement distance, muzzle awareness always.
